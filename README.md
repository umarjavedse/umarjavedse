### 🚀 About me

I'm a full stack engineer with **9+ years** of building production web apps in TypeScript, Node.js, React and PostgreSQL. These days most of my work is on **AI and LLM-powered products**.

- 💼 **Principal Software Engineer at Mavric**, where I lead a team of 5 engineers
- 🤖 Leading engineering of **CommerceAssist AI**, a support assistant for online stores that answers shoppers from each store's own policies and FAQs, with sources
- ⚡ Moved the team to a **spec-driven workflow with Claude Code and Cursor sub-agents**, shipping about **40% faster**
- 🐞 Cut debugging time roughly in half by pairing **Sentry with LLM-based root-cause analysis**
- 🌍 Built multi-tenant SaaS products for international clients in e-commerce, education, HR and analytics
- 🏆 **Coaching Champ award** at Mavric for training and mentoring the team
